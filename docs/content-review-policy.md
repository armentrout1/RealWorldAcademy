# Content Review Policy

## Purpose

Real World Academy should let people be creative while keeping family trust. This policy defines the review standard for submitted lessons, resources, and curriculum collections.

## Review Principle

Creative content can be welcomed. Student-facing publication must be earned through review.

No contributor submission should appear publicly until it has been reviewed for safety, age fit, source quality, originality, learning usefulness, clarity, and credential fit.

## Applies To

- Lesson submissions.
- Resource submissions.
- Curriculum collections.
- External videos and links.
- Parent notes and student prompts.
- Final projects.
- Future AI-assisted curriculum drafts.

## Review Outcomes

### Approved

Use when content is safe enough for beta, useful for learning, and clear enough for families.

### Changes Requested

Use when the idea is useful but needs edits before publication.

Common reasons:

- Needs clearer parent guidance.
- Age level is mismatched.
- External source needs more context.
- Activity is too vague.
- Credential fit is unclear.
- Safety note is missing.

### Rejected

Use when content should not be published.

Common reasons:

- Unsafe advice.
- Unsupported health, legal, financial, or mental health claims.
- Plagiarized or rights-unclear content.
- Inappropriate student-facing material.
- Misleading credential or accreditation claim.
- Too low-quality to repair with reasonable edits.

### Archived

Use when content is no longer active, no longer relevant, duplicated, outdated, or removed after a report.

## Rubric

### Safety

Check for:

- Age-appropriate topic handling.
- No dangerous instructions without adult guidance.
- No professional advice represented as instruction.
- Clear warnings around money, health, relationships, online behavior, tools, and external websites.

### Age Fit

Check for:

- Vocabulary matches the age group.
- Examples match student maturity.
- Activity can reasonably be completed by the stated age group.
- Parent involvement is clear where needed.

### Source Trust

Check for:

- External resources are credible enough for beta.
- YouTube/video links are relevant and not misleading.
- Source labels are present.
- External content does not undermine family trust.

### Originality and Rights

Check for:

- Contributor appears to have written the submission.
- Quoted or copied material is not excessive.
- External resources are linked rather than pasted wholesale.
- Worksheets, PDFs, videos, and images have permission or are avoided.

### Learning Usefulness

Check for:

- Clear objective.
- Real-world scenario or application.
- Activity that produces evidence of learning.
- Reflection prompt or parent/student discussion point.

### Clarity

Check for:

- Family can understand what to do.
- Student can follow the lesson.
- Parent knows how to support or review it.
- Collection steps are ordered logically.

### Credential Fit

Check for:

- Content does not claim official credit.
- If credential eligible, completion evidence is clear.
- Requirements can be explained to families.
- Parent review or final project evidence is available where needed.

## External Video Rules

- Prefer videos from sources that are stable and appropriate.
- Add source label, duration, parent prompt, and safety notes when possible.
- Do not imply Real World Academy controls external content.
- Families should be reminded that external content can change.

## Report Handling

When a family reports content:

1. Mark report as `reviewing`.
2. Open the reported content.
3. Check the report category and message.
4. Decide whether to leave, edit, request changes, archive, or remove the content.
5. Record action taken.
6. Mark the report `resolved` or `archived`.

## Public Trust Standard

If an admin would not be comfortable explaining the content to a parent, it should not be approved yet.
