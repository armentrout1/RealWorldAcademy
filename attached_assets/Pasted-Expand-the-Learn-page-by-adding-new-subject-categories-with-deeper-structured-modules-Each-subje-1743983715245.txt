Expand the “Learn” page by adding new subject categories with deeper, structured modules. Each subject should be real-world focused and scaffolded by age group.

1. **Add 4 New Subject Cards to the Learn Page**:
   Add these new cards alongside the existing ones (Math, Science, etc.):
   - 🗣️ **Communication & Relationships**  
     “Learn how to express yourself, listen, and work well with others.”
   - 🧘 **Health & Wellness**  
     “Understand your body, your mind, and how to take care of both.”
   - 💼 **Entrepreneurship & Work Skills**  
     “Start a hustle, prep for jobs, and build skills employers want.”
   - 💻 **Tech in the Real World**  
     “Explore tools, apps, and digital skills used by real professionals.”

Each card should follow the same style: title, icon/image, 1-sentence real-world use, and a “Learn More” button.

2. **Create Module Pages for Each Subject**:
   For each subject, create a dedicated module page with 3–4 lessons, divided by **age group tiers**:
   - Tier 1: Ages 9–12 (Beginner concepts)
   - Tier 2: Ages 13–15 (Intermediate real-world use)
   - Tier 3: Ages 16–18 (Deeper applications, independence)

Each lesson should include:
   - A title (e.g., “Active Listening 101” or “How Credit Cards Actually Work”)
   - 1–2 paragraph explanation
   - Optional: image or diagram
   - 1 interactive component (button to generate example, a multiple-choice question, or a “Think About It” reflection box)

3. **Age Group Selector Component**:
   On each subject page, include a toggle or dropdown to select their age group so content adjusts accordingly.

4. **Add Sample Content to Each New Subject**:

**Example for Communication & Relationships:**
   - Tier 1: “Using Kind Words”
   - Tier 2: “Handling Arguments With Respect”
   - Tier 3: “How to Speak in a Job Interview”

**Example for Health & Wellness:**
   - Tier 1: “What Is Sleep and Why It Matters”
   - Tier 2: “Eating for Energy”
   - Tier 3: “Mental Health Basics and Boundaries”

**Example for Entrepreneurship:**
   - Tier 1: “What is a Business?”
   - Tier 2: “How to Price Something You Sell”
   - Tier 3: “Starting a Side Hustle or Micro-Business”

**Example for Tech in the Real World:**
   - Tier 1: “What is the Cloud?”
   - Tier 2: “Using Google Docs & Spreadsheets”
   - Tier 3: “How to Build a Simple Website”

5. **Link Progress to Tracker**:
   For each completed lesson, trigger a visual update on the progress tracker created in Phase 10 (can be static JS for now).

6. **Make Everything Mobile-Friendly and Engaging**:
   - Use icons, soft color blocks, and collapsible sections
   - Support touch interactions for younger learners using tablets

This phase makes the platform deeper, smarter, and more powerful — while still feeling playful and personal for every kid using it.
