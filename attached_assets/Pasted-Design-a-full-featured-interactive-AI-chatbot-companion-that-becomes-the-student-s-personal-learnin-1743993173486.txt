Design a full-featured, interactive AI chatbot companion that becomes the student's personal learning partner, guide, and mentor. This is more than a chat window — it’s a relationship-driven, emotionally supportive AI buddy designed for kids.

1. **Create a Dedicated Chatbot Interface Panel**:
   - Add a “Buddy” icon or avatar button on the dashboard (e.g., bottom-left corner)
   - When clicked, open a full chatbot panel with:
     - AI name and avatar (customizable)
     - Welcome message
     - Message history area
     - Input box to chat
     - Dropdown settings menu (see below)

2. **Design the Chatbot Avatar with Personality**:
   - Give the chatbot a fun, friendly name like “Buddy,” “Luma,” “Riley,” or let the student rename it
   - Let students choose from 2–3 starter avatars (animal, robot, human-like)
   - Allow students to:
     - Pick voice/tone style (e.g., “Funny,” “Calm,” “Motivator”)
     - Customize colors or themes
     - Name their buddy (“This is MY version of Buddy”)

3. **Add a Login Panel for the Chatbot**:
   - Just like the student has a login, create a Buddy login screen
     - Shows “Welcome back, [student name] — Buddy is ready to help!”
     - Button to “Open Buddy” (goes to chat window)
     - Settings dropdown:
       - Change Buddy Appearance
       - Buddy’s Personality Style
       - Reset Buddy
       - Privacy / Chat Logs

4. **Simulate Key AI Behaviors (Placeholder Logic for Now)**:
   - Memory: Buddy “remembers” recent interactions and repeats them back
     - “Last time you said you wanted to be a designer — want to try a new project today?”
   - Emotion Check-In:
     - “How are you feeling today?” (happy, bored, stressed, excited)
     - Respond with empathy and a suggestion: “Let’s do something creative to lift your mood!”
   - Creative Collaboration:
     - “Want to co-write a story?” or “Let’s design your dream room.”
     - Show typing prompts or generate random ideas
   - Reflection & Motivation:
     - “What’s something you’re proud of this week?”
     - “Here’s a quote to inspire you!”

5. **Add Chat History & Interaction Styles**:
   - Display past conversations in scrollable chat log
   - Different message types:
     - Text
     - Emoji reactions
     - Card replies (e.g., “Pick a topic: 🌍 Travel | 🎮 Gaming | 💰 Money Tips”)

6. **Long-Term Growth & Bonding Features**:
   - Show “Buddy Growth Bar” or “Trust Meter” that fills as they interact
   - Unlock new Buddy traits or visuals as they progress
   - Optional journal view that shows what they’ve told Buddy before

7. **Design for Safety & Age Appropriateness**:
   - Add content filtering placeholders
   - Simulate "safe responses only" logic
   - Add parent control in settings: “Buddy Reports Summary to Parent Weekly”
   - Visible disclaimer: “Buddy is your learning partner, not a real person. Be kind.”

8. **Design & UX Notes**:
   - Friendly colors, soft UI, bubbly feel
   - Full-screen on mobile for better immersion
   - Smooth open/close transitions
   - Works alongside other platform features — Buddy should feel like part of the world
