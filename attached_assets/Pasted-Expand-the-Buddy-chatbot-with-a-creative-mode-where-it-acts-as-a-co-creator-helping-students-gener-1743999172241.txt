Expand the Buddy chatbot with a creative mode where it acts as a co-creator — helping students generate stories, build business ideas, imagine the future, or design cool projects.

1. **Add a “Create With Me” Button in Buddy’s Chat Panel**:
   - When clicked, show a menu of creative prompts:
     - ✍️ “Let’s Write a Story”
     - 🧠 “Invent Something New”
     - 💼 “Start a Business”
     - 🌎 “Design Your Dream Future”
     - 🔁 “Surprise Me!”

2. **Let Students Choose the Creative Mode**:

**✍️ Story Builder**
- Buddy asks: “What kind of story do you want to write?”
- Options: Adventure, Funny, Mystery, Real Life, Fantasy
- Asks follow-ups:
  - Who’s your main character?
  - Where does the story start?
  - What’s the twist?
- Builds paragraphs together with student input

**🧠 Invention Generator**
- Prompts like:
  - “Let’s create something the world hasn’t seen yet!”
  - “What problem do you want to solve?”
- Buddy asks questions and responds like a brainstorming partner
- Creates a visual or description of the invention

**💼 Mini Business Builder**
- Prompts like:
  - “Let’s build your first hustle!”
  - “What would you love to sell or offer?”
- Walks through steps:
  - What’s the idea?
  - Who needs it?
  - How much will it cost?
  - What should we call it?

**🌎 Future You Dreamer**
- Prompts like:
  - “Fast forward 10 years… what’s your dream life look like?”
  - “Where are you living? What’s your job? What do you love about it?”
- Buddy helps build a life vision (can be added to journal)

**🔁 Surprise Me**
- Buddy picks a fun random prompt:
  - “Design a new holiday”
  - “Make a superhero for your school”
  - “What if your pet could talk?”

3. **Show Progress + Save to Journal**:
   - After each creative activity, Buddy says:
     - “Want to save this to your Journal?”
     - “Should we keep building it later?”

4. **Creative Mode Style Tips**:
   - Add a soft, “imaginative” background or visual toggle
   - Emoji-rich, friendly voice
   - Option to use a whiteboard or canvas area (placeholder)

5. **Bonus: Unlock Creative Badges**
   - “First Story Created”  
   - “Inventor Mode Activated”  
   - “Dream Builder”  
   - Add XP for each creative session

Make this space fun, open-ended, and judgment-free — it's about **expression**, not perfection.
