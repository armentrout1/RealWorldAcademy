# Terminology and Claims Guide

## Purpose

Real World Academy needs language that is inspiring without overclaiming. This guide keeps copy consistent across product pages, admin workflows, contributor tools, credentials, safety pages, and public beta materials.

## Core Public Terms

Use these terms often:

- `homeschool support`
- `real-world learning`
- `factual learning`
- `practical learning`
- `learning path`
- `curriculum collection`
- `lesson`
- `class`
- `live class`
- `recorded class`
- `teacher`
- `educator`
- `tutor`
- `mentor`
- `project`
- `portfolio`
- `completion credential`
- `parent review`
- `reviewed contributor content`
- `reviewed educator content`
- `curated resource`
- `external resource`
- `family-guided learning`
- `educator profile`
- `teacher profile`
- `learning service`

## Terms To Use Carefully

Use only with clear context:

- `credential`: pair with `completion`, `platform`, or `non-accredited` when public-facing.
- `curriculum`: okay for reviewed learning paths, but avoid implying state-approved curriculum.
- `school`: okay in the product name, but avoid claiming the platform is an accredited school.
- `teacher`: okay when a person is teaching, tutoring, or offering classes. Avoid implying state employment, licensure, or school accreditation unless verified.
- `educator`: broader than teacher; useful for content creators, tutors, mentors, trade instructors, cooking instructors, finance educators, and subject experts.
- `creator`: useful for people who create curriculum, videos, resources, or classes but may not be traditional teachers.
- `marketplace`: okay for paid educator offerings, but do not make the student experience feel like an uncontrolled gig site.
- `certified`: avoid unless a real certification process exists.
- `verified`: use only for verified account or source checks that are actually implemented.

## Terms To Avoid In Public Claims

Avoid these unless legal/business review and product evidence support them:

- `accredited`
- `official school credit`
- `state-approved`
- `guaranteed transcript`
- `certified graduate`
- `teacher-approved` unless reviewed by named qualified educators
- `licensed teacher` unless verified
- `licensed curriculum`
- `college-ready guarantee`
- `job-ready guarantee`
- `replaces school`
- `meets homeschool requirements in every state`

## Credential Copy Rules

Good:

`This credential records completion of Real World Academy requirements.`

Better:

`This non-accredited completion credential shows the lessons, projects, and parent-reviewed work completed in Real World Academy.`

Avoid:

`Earn official credit.`

Avoid:

`Become certified in financial literacy.`

## Contributor Copy Rules

Good:

`Submit a lesson, resource, or curriculum collection for review.`

Good:

`Approved content may be published for families to use.`

Avoid:

`Post anything instantly.`

Avoid:

`Become a certified Real World Academy teacher.`

## Educator Marketplace Copy Rules

Good:

`Create a teacher profile, share free sample lessons, and submit paid classes for review.`

Good:

`Educators can offer live classes, recorded lessons, tutoring, coaching, or curriculum collections after platform approval.`

Good:

`Real World Academy may collect a platform fee from paid educator offerings.`

Avoid:

`Teach anything with no review.`

Avoid:

`All teachers are licensed and verified.`

Avoid:

`Guaranteed results for every student.`

Avoid:

`Official school classes for credit.`

## Family Safety Copy Rules

Good:

`External resources can change. Parents should review videos and links before assigning them to younger students.`

Good:

`Report inaccurate, unsafe, broken, or age-mismatched content so admins can review it.`

Avoid:

`All videos are guaranteed safe.`

Avoid:

`This lesson replaces professional advice.`

## AI Copy Rules

If AI features are exposed publicly, use assistive language:

- `AI-assisted`
- `draft`
- `suggestion`
- `starting point`
- `review before using`

Avoid:

- `AI-approved`
- `guaranteed personalized curriculum`
- `automatic teacher`
- `expert diagnosis`

## Public Disclaimer Pattern

Use this pattern near credentials, safety, and public launch copy:

`Real World Academy is a homeschool support and practical skills learning platform. It is not an accredited school and does not grant official academic credit. Families remain responsible for local homeschool requirements and for reviewing external resources before student use.`

## Marketplace Disclaimer Pattern

Use this pattern near paid educator services:

`Educator profiles, classes, and learning services are offered by independent teachers, tutors, mentors, or creators. Real World Academy provides the platform, review process, payment flow, and safety rules, but families remain responsible for choosing what fits their learner.`

## Internal Naming Notes

Backend names do not need to be renamed just to match public copy. Public terminology should improve user trust first. Database tables, route names, and older component names can be changed only during scoped refactor phases.
